Notes on optical metrology — thin-film and surface measurement, computational optics, and the numerical methods that turn raw signals into physical quantities.

- **Optics and physics**: thin-film and surface metrology, spectroscopic reflectometry, ellipsometry (rotating-compensator, Mueller matrix, channeled and snapshot configurations)
- **Computation and algorithms**: numerical methods and optimization, image processing, machine learning combined with physics-based forward models, in Python and C++
- **Surface analysis**: XPS and related techniques — photoemission, surface sensitivity, quantification, and depth profiling

I spent eight years building optical metrology instruments end to end: hardware, alignment and calibration, and the analysis software that goes with them. Most of that work ended up in papers, patents, and lab code that nobody else could read. This blog is an attempt to write it down properly.

Posts are written in Korean first. English versions are added for the ones likely to be useful beyond Korea.
