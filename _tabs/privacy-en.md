This page describes how this site (`https://eddykim.github.io`) handles visitor information, and the terms under which the writing and code published here may be used.

This is a personal technical notebook. There is no sign-up or login, and no form collects names, contact details, or similar personal information. Everything listed below is handled automatically by external services.

## 1. Analytics

The following tools are used to understand which posts are read.

| Tool | Collected | Cookies |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [GoatCounter](https://www.goatcounter.com/) | Page path, referrer, browser and screen size, as anonymous aggregates | Not used |
| [Google Analytics 4](https://policies.google.com/privacy) | Page path, referrer, approximate location, device information, a visitor identifier, and interactions such as scroll depth, outbound link clicks, and file downloads | Used |

GoatCounter sets no cookies and does not identify individuals. Google Analytics 4 does set a cookie to distinguish visitors, and with "enhanced measurement" enabled it also records interactions beyond page views. Data collected by Google Analytics is processed under Google's privacy policy; no personally identifying information such as a name or contact address is collected.

You may opt out through your browser's cookie settings or the [Google Analytics opt-out add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout). Opting out places no restriction on reading anything here.

These statistics are used only to see which posts are read. They are not sold or supplied to third parties.

## 2. Comments

Comments are stored in GitHub Discussions through [giscus](https://giscus.app). This site keeps no separate database for them; the content and account information belong to GitHub and are subject to the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement).

Posting a comment requires authorising the giscus app with a GitHub account. That authorisation exists so the comment can be posted under your own account; the site owner has no access to your GitHub credentials. You can edit or delete your own comments directly in the repository's Discussions.

## 3. External resources

Rendering a page pulls fonts, mathematics rendering (MathJax), and diagrams (Mermaid) from Google Fonts and the jsDelivr CDN. Your IP address may be visible to those services as a result.

## 4. Advertising

There is currently no advertising on this site. Should that change, this page will be updated first, together with a note on whether the advertising provider uses cookies and how to opt out.

This covers inclusion in a training corpus as well as inference and grounding (RAG) uses that contribute to generated output.
